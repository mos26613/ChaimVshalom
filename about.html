About Us

Our Mission

At Chaim V'Shalom Women's Learning Center, our mission is to create a warm, welcoming space where women of Ramat Beit Shemesh and surrounding communities can come together to learn, grow, and connect. Through inspiring Torah classes, meaningful discussions, and enriching experiences, we strive to nurture spiritual growth, build lasting friendships, and foster a true sense of belonging. Our goal is to create a place where every woman feels comfortable, valued, and inspired.

Our Story

Chaim V'Shalom was born from a dream to create something special for the women of our community — a place that offers not only meaningful Torah learning, but also opportunities for friendship, creativity, and connection. What began as a vision for a learning program is the beginning of something much greater: a community center where women can feel at home, discover new interests, and grow together.

Our Vision

Chaim V'Shalom was born from a dream to create a place where the community can come together to learn, grow, connect, and feel a true sense of belonging.

Our vision is to offer a wide variety of meaningful classes and enriching experiences tailored to the values and interests of our community in Ramat Beit Shemesh, the surrounding areas, and beyond.

But our dream extends beyond the classroom. We envision a vibrant center that nurtures creativity through art and music, encourages friendships through social activities and games, and provides opportunities to build lasting connections.

Above all, we want to create a warm, comfortable, and welcoming environment — a place where everyone feels at home, where no one needs to feel alone, and where there is always a reason to walk through the door.

Our dream is simple: to create not just a place to learn, but a place to belong.

Founded L'Ilui Nishmas חיים ישעי' בן משה and שלום אליה בן פייוול ראובן, Chaim V'Shalom is a living tribute to their memory, transforming remembrance into a lasting legacy of Torah, inspiration, and community.
